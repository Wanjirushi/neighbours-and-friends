Privacy Policy

Neighbour Friend Finder helps adults meet compatible people in and around their neighbourhoods. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What we collect

When you register, we collect details such as your name, age, phone number, optional email, neighbourhood or selected map location, availability, interests, lifestyle preferences, family or life-stage information, and your M-PESA transaction reference and payment details. We also receive basic website activity information through analytics and advertising technologies.

How we use your information

We use your details to process and verify membership, identify potential friendship matches and groups, contact you about introductions, provide weekly membership updates, invite you to community activities, respond to requests, maintain records, and protect the service from misuse. We use Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to understand visits, registrations and verified purchases and measure advertising performance.

Location and introductions

We use the neighbourhood and location information you choose to help find people nearby. We do not need to publish your precise map coordinates to other members. When arranging an introduction, we may share selected profile details and contact information with the people involved so they can connect. We aim to share only what is needed for that introduction.

WhatsApp communication

WhatsApp is our primary membership communication channel. We may contact you there about payment verification, introductions, matching progress and relevant events. WhatsApp and its service providers may process communications according to their own policies. You can request that we stop contacting you or delete your membership, although that may prevent us from providing the service.

Advertising and service providers

We use service providers for website hosting, secure database storage, mapping and messaging. We use Meta advertising tools to measure the effectiveness of our ads. These tools may receive technical information about your browser and events on the website. For verified purchases, matching information such as phone numbers or email addresses may be hashed before being sent to Meta. Hashing does not make the information anonymous to Meta. We do not send your lifestyle answers, exact location or M-PESA transaction code to Meta as advertising event details.

Keeping information safe

We use access controls and technical safeguards intended to protect member records. No online service can promise absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for membership, legitimate business records and applicable legal obligations, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, subject to applicable legal obligations. You may also adjust browser privacy settings to limit advertising tracking. To make a privacy request, contact Neighbour Friend Finder through its official membership WhatsApp channel. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Updates

We may update this notice as our service evolves. We will publish the revised policy here.

For deletion instructions, see our User Data Deletion page. For membership terms, see our Terms of Service.
