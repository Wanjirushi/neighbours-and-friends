User Data Deletion

Last updated: 17 October 2026

You can request that Neighbour Friend Finder delete your membership profile and associated personal information. You do not need to have an active membership to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Open our official WhatsApp channel: +254 759 074804.
  2. Send the message: “I would like to request deletion of my Neighbour Friend Finder account and personal data.”
  3. Include the phone number you used when registering, if different from the number you are messaging from. Do not send your M-PESA PIN or other passwords.
  4. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before proceeding.

What happens next

After verifying your request, we will stop active matchmaking and remove or de-identify personal information held for your member profile, such as contact details, preferences, matching records and related communications, where we can lawfully do so. We will also review pending introductions and group participation as appropriate. We will acknowledge your request and explain the outcome and any information that must be retained. We aim to respond promptly and within applicable legal timeframes.

Information we may need to retain

Some limited records may need to be kept for payment reconciliation, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legal obligations. We will restrict such records to those purposes and retain them only as long as necessary. Messages or contact details already shared with other members, or information held independently by WhatsApp or other providers, may not be fully retrievable or erasable by us.

Other privacy requests

You can use the same contact channel to request access to or correction of your information, or to ask us to stop certain communications. See our Privacy Policy for further details.
