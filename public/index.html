A few things you might be wondering

Frequently asked questions

How does Neighbour Friend Finder work?

After your payment is confirmed, we review your answers and introduce you individually to compatible people near you. We prioritise location, age, lifestyle and other preferences. Where several members in the same area are highly compatible, we may also form a WhatsApp friendship group.

How close will my matches be?

Individual matches are normally within a 5 km radius of your selected location. We also consider a similar age group and compatible lifestyles. For example, we match non-drinkers with non-drinkers.

How many friends will I be introduced to?

You can receive up to 15 unique individual introductions during your one-year membership, depending on who is available and compatible. We initially work towards five introductions. We do not force unsuitable matches.

How much does membership cost, and how long is it valid?

Membership costs KSh 2,800 and is valid for one year.

What happens after I pay?

Enter your M-PESA transaction code in the form. Once we confirm your payment, your profile is submitted to our member database for matchmaking.

What if there are no compatible members nearby yet?

We give you a weekly update on your account status until your first five highly compatible matches have been introduced. After five introductions, we give you time to get to know those neighbours. If you choose to continue matching, we resume introductions towards your next five neighbour matches. In the very unlikely event that we cannot find any compatible matches—for example, if you live in a remote area such as Turkana—we will contact you and offer a choice: a refund, extending your membership for another year, or expanding your matching radius to reach the nearest compatible person, with your agreement.

Does this service include both men and women?

Yes. Both men and women are welcome to join Neighbour Friend Finder. We make individual introductions based on location, age, lifestyle preferences and compatibility.

Are there community events too?

Yes. Members receive monthly invitations to affordable or free hangouts in their greater area, such as picnics or group walks. These events offer another way to meet people, including those just outside your usual 5 km matching radius.
