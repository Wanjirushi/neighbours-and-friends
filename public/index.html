Neighbours and Friends
How it works ↓

Making friends as an adult is hard.

After school ends, the built-in, everyday relationships end too.

There is no more “see you tomorrow.”

No more sharing lunch.

No more walking together at the end of the day.

No more seeing the same people so often that friendship happens without either of you having to plan it.

We’re here to make that possible again.

Our matching system connects you with people close to you who are looking for the same kind of friendship you are.

Because sometimes, the human connection we need is as simple as sharing a meal together.

Or taking a 10-minute walk while talking about anything and nothing in particular.

Sometimes, a face-to-face chat with just one person, once a week, is all we need.

Someone who remembers what you told them last week and asks, “How is that thing you were telling me about going?”

Someone who notices when you haven’t been around.

Someone you can call when you feel like getting out of the house.

Someone you can meet in five minutes without needing to be dressed up.

Someone who can pop by and share a cup of tea.

No big production. Just a person connecting with another.

Where neighbours become friends and friends become family.
