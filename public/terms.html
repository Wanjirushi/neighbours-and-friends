These terms explain the Neighbour Friend Finder membership service. Please read them before registering and paying. By registering and using the service, you agree to these terms, subject to applicable Kenyan law.

Who can join

Neighbour Friend Finder is an adult friendship-matching service. You must be at least 18 years old, provide accurate registration information and use the service for genuine social friendship. We may refuse or suspend access where reasonably necessary to protect members or prevent misuse.

Membership and payment

Membership costs KSh 2,800 for one year, starting when your payment is verified. You submit your M-PESA transaction reference for manual verification. Membership includes an ongoing search for compatible individual introductions, up to 15 unique introductions during the membership year depending on availability and compatibility, and compatible WhatsApp groups where applicable. We initially work towards five individual introductions. The fee does not purchase a guaranteed number of friendships or guarantee a specific match.

Matching and introductions

We prioritise your chosen location (normally within approximately 5 km), age range, availability, interests, lifestyle and other preferences. We may suggest a match outside your preferred area and ask whether you wish to proceed. The availability of compatible people varies by area and over time; we do not force unsuitable matches. We aim to begin making suitable introductions as members become available, but cannot promise an exact date or outcome. While your search remains active, we aim to provide weekly matching progress updates.

If no introduction is made

If we cannot make a single suitable introduction during your membership year, we will offer you a membership rollover or a refund, consistent with the commitment published on our website. Contact us using our official WhatsApp number to request a review. This does not limit any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law.

Community events

We may organise or invite members to local meetups, walks, picnics and other activities. Invitations and event availability are not guaranteed for every location or month. Event participation, transport, food, venue charges and other personal expenses may be separate from the membership fee and will be communicated where applicable.

Respect and safety

Treat other members respectfully. Harassment, threats, discrimination, fraud, unsolicited commercial approaches, impersonation and sharing another member's information without permission are not allowed. You are responsible for your own decisions about meeting people. For first meetings, we recommend public places and informing someone you trust of your plans. Neighbour Friend Finder facilitates introductions; it does not supervise private meetings or guarantee another person's conduct.

WhatsApp and your information

WhatsApp is the primary channel for membership updates, payment confirmation and introductions. We may share limited relevant profile details or contact information with a proposed match when arranging an introduction. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information. You may request data deletion using our Data Deletion page.

Cancellation, complaints and changes

You can ask to stop receiving messages or close your membership through our official WhatsApp channel. Refund requests and complaints are reviewed in light of the service provided, the no-introduction commitment above and applicable Kenyan law.
